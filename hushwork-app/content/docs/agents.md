# Agents: describe it and it runs

An agent is a task you describe in one sentence. Hushwork saves it and runs it straight away, and on a schedule if you say when.

## Make an agent

1. Open **Agents** in the side menu.
2. Describe the task in plain English, for example "Every weekday at 9, sum up today's meetings and email them to me".
3. Press **Create agent**.

Hushwork writes the steps for you with the same planner as the workspace, saves the agent, and runs it once. You land on that run, with the results under each step.

## Scheduling is optional

If your sentence says when, like "every Monday at 9" or "every weekday at 17:30", the agent also runs on that schedule, in your own time. If it does not say when, it simply runs now, and you can press **Run now** on its card whenever you like.

To add, change or pause a schedule later, use the schedule panel on the agent's card. See [Schedules](/docs/schedules) for how the clock works.

## What an agent does by itself

- It reads, looks things up and asks the AI by itself.
- A step that sends an email, adds a row or creates a task waits for your **Approve** on the Runs page, exactly like a Manual run. You read the exact words first.
- A scheduled agent only reads by itself until you tick **Let it write by itself** on its schedule.

See [Runs and Approve](/docs/runs-and-approvals) for how approving works.

## Agents and the Builder

An agent is a workflow underneath. Click its name to open it in the Builder and change any step by hand. Your other workflows stay on the **Workflows** page, and the Builder works the same as before. Use whichever you like: describe it as an agent, or build it block by block.

## Agents and your Hushwork Agent

[Your Hushwork Agent](/docs/agent) is different: it is always on and leaves notes on your workspace. The agents on the Agents page are the ones you create yourself, each for one task.
