# The Builder and its blocks

The Builder is where you change a workflow: add blocks, fill in the blanks, put the steps in order and press Save.

## How to get here

Every way of starting ends in the same Builder:

- A plan from the Workspace: press **Open in the Builder**.
- A template: press **Open in the Builder** on a template card.
- A skill: pick one from the **Skills** list on the left.
- A saved workflow: click its name on the **Workflows** page.
- Or press **New in the Builder** on the Workflows page to start empty.

## The screen

At the top is **Name your workflow**. On the left are the **Blocks**. On the right is **Your workflow**, the list of steps. A workflow can have up to **12 steps**.

## Add, edit, move, delete

- **Add a step:** drag a block into your workflow, or just press it. Pressing adds it at the end, which also works on phones and with a keyboard.
- **Edit a step:** fill in the blanks on the step. Each blank has a hint.
- **Move a step:** use the **up and down arrows** on the step, or drag it by the grip on its left.
- **Delete a step:** press the bin icon on the step.
- **Save:** press **Save** (or **Save changes** for a saved workflow). Unsaved changes are lost if you leave the page.

> **Careful:** Do not put passwords or card numbers in a step.

## The blocks, in one line each

| Block | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Start | Says how the workflow begins: When I ask, On a schedule, or Keep watching. |
| Linear | Looks at your open Linear tasks. |
| Web address | Asks one website for information (read only). |
| Ask the AI | Gives the AI a job. It can look at one place first, then write. It only reads. |
| MCP server | Lets the AI use the read-only tools of one tool server. |
| Ask me first | A stop sign that marks where writing starts. |
| Add a row | Adds one line to a Google Sheet you name. |
| Create Linear task | Adds one new task in Linear. |
| Another app | Looks at something in an app connected through Composio. Read only. |
| Do in another app | Really sends an email or Slack message, creates an issue or a page, and more. |
| Note | Your own words. It does nothing when run. |

Older plans may also hold three "plan only" blocks (Find something, Write something, Send a message). They only describe things and do nothing, so the Builder no longer offers them. See [Step types](/docs/step-types) for every kind in detail.

## Skills and emailing several people

The **Skills** list on the left adds a ready-made step. Each shows a note like "Reads only", "AI writes text" or "Writes: needs Approve". A skill that writes brings an **Ask me first** step with it. The note "Needs..." means an app is not connected yet.

**Email several people** makes one separate email step for each address you type, up to 8, so you approve each email on its own.

## Step cards

Under each step is a small card. **Why** says why the step is there. **Evidence** says Planned until the step has run. If a step needs an app you have not connected, the card says so and offers **Connect**, which opens in a new tab so your work is not lost.

## Plan-only warning

If a step would run but do nothing, Hushwork warns you when you save. To really send or create something, use **Do in another app**.

## Try it

1. Open the Builder and name it "Test".
2. Press the **Start** block, then the **Ask the AI** block.
3. Move the AI step up and down with the arrows.
4. Press **Save**.
