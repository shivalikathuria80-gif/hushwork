# Glossary

Short plain-words meanings for the words you meet in Hushwork.

## Words about using Hushwork

- **Workflow** - a job you describe once and can run again. It is a list of steps, saved under the name you give it.
- **Step** - one small piece of a workflow, such as "look at my calendar" or "send an email".
- **Draft** - a saved plan in Hushwork. A draft is only a plan to read. It is never sent anywhere.
- **Builder** - the page where you drag blocks into a plan and fill in their boxes.
- **Block** - one kind of step you can add in the Builder, such as Start or Ask the AI.
- **Template** - a ready-made workflow you can open in the Builder and change.
- **Skill** - one ready-made step you can add from the Skills list in the Builder.
- **Bundle** - a package of ready-made workflows on the Skills page. Turning one on saves its workflows. Nothing runs and nothing connects until you do it.
- **Start** - the first step. It says what starts the workflow, such as a schedule or "When I ask".
- **Schedule** - a time rule such as "Every weekday at 17:30". Hushwork then runs the workflow by itself at that time.
- **Watch** - a scheduled check that stays quiet unless something is new. It is made with the Start choice **Keep watching**.
- **Quiet hours** - from 20:00 to 08:00 your time. A watch never speaks up then.
- **Speak-up** - the one plain email a watch sends to tell you something is waiting. It says to open the Runs page. It never holds what was found. At most 3 a day.
- **Memory** - short facts you save in Settings, such as your timezone, that the AI may use as background.

## Words about running and approving

- **Run** - one go at a workflow. You see it on the Runs page.
- **Practice run** - a run that only writes down what each step would do. Nothing leaves Hushwork.
- **Live run** - a record of something that really happened after you pressed Approve, such as an email sent or a task created.
- **Approve** - the button you press to let one step really happen, after reading the exact words on its card.
- **Manual mode** - the normal mode. Every real step waits for your Approve click.
- **Auto mode** - a mode you choose on purpose. It does every real step straight away, in order, without asking.
- **Let it write by itself** - a box on a schedule. If it is ticked, scheduled runs may send or create things while you are away. Otherwise they skip those steps.
- **Plan only** - a label on an old step that only describes what would happen. It never does anything.
- **Not sure if this happened** - a step that was started but whose end Hushwork never saw. It is not sent again by itself. You check the app first.
- **Pause switch** - a switch only the owner can flip that stops writing, AI calls and scheduled runs for everybody.
- **Placeholder** - a word in curly brackets that gets replaced, such as `{answer}`, `{answers}`, `{last}` and `{myEmail}`.
- **Gmail draft** - an email saved in your Gmail Drafts. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

## Words about apps and connections

- **Connection** - a permission you gave Hushwork on another app's own page, so it can look at that app or add one thing to it.
- **Connect / Disconnect** - the buttons that give or take back that permission.
- **Composio** - a separate company that keeps sign-ins for many apps, so Hushwork can reach them. Hushwork never sees those permissions.
- **Test all my connections** - a button on the Connections page that does a real but harmless look at everything you connected.
- **Read only** - able to look but not change.
- **Not available yet** - an app that is listed but cannot be connected yet.
- **MCP** - a standard way for a company to offer tools to an AI. Hushwork only lets the AI use read-only tools of one server you name.

## Words about the technical side

- **AI** - Hushwork's AI is made by Groq, which runs AI models.
- **Groq** - the company that runs the AI model. Text the AI needs goes to Groq.
- **Supabase** - the service that holds Hushwork's database and sign-in.
- **API** - a way for two pieces of software to talk to each other. Hushwork uses other apps' APIs to look at or add things.
- **Token** - a secret code an app gives Hushwork to prove you allowed it. Hushwork keeps the ones it gets scrambled (encrypted).
- **UTC** - the world's standard clock. Daily limits reset at midnight UTC.
- **Test user** - someone the owner added so they can connect Google before Google has reviewed Hushwork.
