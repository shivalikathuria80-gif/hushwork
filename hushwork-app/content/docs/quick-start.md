# Your first 5 minutes

Sign in, describe one chore, look at the plan, try a practice run, and approve one step on the Runs page.

## Before you start

You need an account. Open the sign-in page and choose **Create account** (or **Sign in** if you have one). You enter an email address and a password of at least 10 characters. More about this is on [Your account and your data](/docs/account).

You do not need to connect any app for your first try. A plan can be saved and practiced with nothing connected.

## The five steps

1. **Describe a chore.** On the **Workspace** page, type a chore in the box. Not sure what to type? Tap one of the three example sentences under the box, then press the round arrow button (or press Enter). See [The workspace](/docs/workspace).
2. **Read the plan.** Hushwork shows a list of steps and the words "Nothing will be sent." Nothing runs yet.
3. **Open it in the Builder.** Press **Open in the Builder**. This saves the plan as a draft and opens it where you can change every step. See [The Builder and its blocks](/docs/builder).
4. **Save and practice.** If you changed anything in the Builder, press **Save changes**. Then go to **Workflows** and press **Run**. This is a **practice run**. It lists what each step would do and sends nothing.
5. **Approve one step.** Open the **Runs** page. Each step that does something real shows a card with the exact words. Read it and press **Approve**.

> **Note:** In the default Manual mode, even a step that only reads (like asking the AI) waits for your Approve. A step that needs an app you have not connected tells you so.

## The Getting started card

On the Workspace page you may see a card called **Getting started: 3 small steps**. It ticks itself from what you really did:

1. **Say what you want.** You saved a workflow.
2. **Practice it.** You started a run.
3. **Approve one step.** You approved something on the Runs page.

The card goes away after the third step.

## Try it: a plan that needs nothing

Open **Templates** and pick **Is my website up?**. It is the one ready-made template marked "Works now", because it needs no app. It reads one web page and tells you if it looks like a working site. Change the address in the step to your own site, save it, run it, and approve the AI step on the Runs page.

## Connecting apps

Most useful plans read from an app, like Gmail or Linear. To connect one, open **Connections**. Start with the [Connections overview](/docs/connections). Press **Test all my connections** afterward to see which ones work.

## If the AI box says it is off

If AI planning is off, or you switch off **Write with AI**, Hushwork makes a simpler **keyword preview** in your browser. Your text is not sent to an AI service. You can still save it and open it in the Builder.
