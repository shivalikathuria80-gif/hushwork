Privacy

Last updated 14 October 2026.

What we store

When you join the waitlist we store your email address and the time you joined. Nothing else.

Why

To tell you when early access opens, and to see how many people are interested.

Where it lives

In a database run by Supabase, our database provider. They process it for us and do not use it for anything else.

Who sees it

Only the Hushwork team. We do not sell your email or share it with advertisers.

What this page does not do

  • It has no analytics and no advertising trackers.
  • It remembers your light or dark choice in your own browser. That never leaves your device.
  • Like any website, it loads fonts and code libraries from other servers (Google Fonts, unpkg and the Tailwind CDN). Those servers can see your IP address when your browser asks for them.

Getting your email removed

Write to hello@hushwork.co and we will delete it. We keep it until early access opens or you ask us to remove it, whichever comes first.
