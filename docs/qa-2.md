## Findings
### BLOCKER

### MAJOR

### MINOR
**m1. The secret guard misses ordinary spellings.** `lib/ai/plan.js:56-58`. A 16-digit card typed with two spaces, a newline, dots, slashes, a non-breaking space or full-width digits is sent to Groq; so are `github_pat_...`, AWS `AKIA...` and a `-----BEGIN PRIVATE KEY-----` block (hostile.mjs items 53 to 59, 68 to 70). The privacy page says "looks like", but the guard is the only server-side protection. Fix: test `value.normalize("NFKC")`, use `/\d(?:[\s.\-\/​]{0,2}\d){12,}/`, add `github_pat_|AKIA|-----BEGIN [A-Z ]*PRIVATE KEY` to the key pattern, and add those strings to the "caught" list in `plan.test.js`. (12-digit Aadhaar numbers and SSN stay unflagged; say so on `/privacy` or leave to the warning.)
**m2. A pasted `</task>` can be rebuilt by nesting.** `plan.js:50`. `"</ta</task>sk>"` becomes `"</task>"` after the single-pass removal (closes=2 in hostile.mjs), so `groq.test.js:46` claims more than is true. No blast radius today (no tools, schema-bound answer, shown as text), but it is a defence that does not hold. Fix: `String(text).replace(/[<>]/g, " ")`, or loop the replace until the text stops changing.
**m3. Nothing tests that `previewPlan` checks the user first.** `lib/actions/plan.js:17`. Deleting `await requireUser()` leaves all 66 tests green. Fix: in `secrets.test.js` read the file and assert `"use server"` is first and `requireUser(` appears before `checkPrompt(` and `getGroqKey(` in `previewPlan`.
**m4. "No AI was used" sits under notices that say the AI was asked.** `Composer.js:225`. After a timeout, unusable reply, refusal, 400 or 5xx the text WAS sent to Groq, yet the plan card says "Matched on keywords in your text. No AI was used." Fix: when `view.notice` exists and the reason is not off/sensitive/limit/offline, use "Matched on keywords in your text." (drop the second sentence); simplest is to drop it whenever a notice is shown.
**m5. A slow AI call delays Save, rename, delete and sign out, and nothing says so.** Server Actions are queued by Next ("Server Actions are queued", `node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/02-guides/backend-for-frontend.md:902`). The slow state offers "Show keyword preview instead" (`Composer.js:190`), but a Save pressed next waits for the abandoned AI call (up to 15 s) while the button says "Saving...". Acceptable for now (no hang, 15 s ceiling, AI optional). Fix: add one sentence to the README "AI plans" section; if it ever matters, send the preview through a route handler instead of an action.
**m6. The approval rule has holes in both directions.** `plan.js:85`. No approval step for notify, forward, message, submit, transfer, book, cancel, upload, move, update, text, and an icon of `Send` with neutral words is ignored (limits-model.mjs section E). It also adds one before "Find client emails", "Check payroll sheet", "Look in shared folder". Fix: also trigger when `icon` is `Send` or `Mail`, add `notify|forward|message|submit|transfer|book|cancel|upload|text` to the pattern, and add them to the test list. Low risk now (nothing runs).
**m7. Three accounts can use up the shared AI allowance for everyone.** `limits.js` / `preview.js`: 100 accounts at one a minute reached exactly 60 fetches (the global day cap), after which everyone gets the keyword preview. This is the design (global ceiling) and sign-up is open with Confirm email off. Owner decision, and a line for the "before any deployment" list: per-IP limit or confirmed emails.
